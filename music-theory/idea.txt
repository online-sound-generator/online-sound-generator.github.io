I need a web project (static site) that helps me make music

The main idea is that i position the notes: C, C, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#, A, A#, B
in a circle

I will have a circle and some points like the point where are the hours in a clock so 
C will be put where the 12 hour is on an analog clock
C# will be 1
D will be 2
D# will be 3 and so on until B will be 11


We have the idea of whole step (W) and half step (H)

Major scale: W W H W W W H
Minor scale: W H W W H W W

I will have lines in different colors to connect
Dominant (D) where it has 7H (example: line between C-G)
Subdominant (SD) where it has 5H
Mediant (M) -> 4H
Submediant (SM) -> 9H
SuperTonic (ST) -> 1H

Leading Tone for major scales (LT) it has 1H diff
Subtonic for minor scales (ST) it has 2H diff

Tonic (T) is like the same note one octave higher like C4-C5

Those lines can be toggled on/off

Also we will have intervals

We will also have a way to represent the specific intervals
Major(M), Minor (m), Perfect (P) => (m2 = 1H, M2 = 2H, m3 = 3H, M3 = 4H, P4 = 5H, P5 = 7H, m6 = 8H, M6 = 9H, m7 = 10H, M7 = 11H, P8 = 12H)

We also have augumented intervals
M, P + 1H => Aug

Aug2 = 3H, Aug3 = 5H, Aug4 = 6H, Aug5 = 8H, Aug6 = 10H, Aug7 = 12H, Aug8 = 13H

We also have diminished intervals
m, P - 1H => Dim

Dim2 = 0H, Dim3 = 2H, Dim4 = 4H, Dim5 = 6H, Dim6 = 7H, Dim7 = 9H, Dim8 = 11H

We also have the notion of interval inversion 
here you take the base note and put it 1 octave (12H) higher 


example: C4-G4 (P5[7H]) => G4-C5 (P4[5H])

We also have the notion of triads and chords


We will have a way to visualize the relationship between those in a graphic way on the circle using colored lines
