Polyphonic Tune Generator



Instructions:

Enter notes in the textarea input. Each note is defined using the following format:

  • Note: A letter from A to G representing the musical note.
  • Sharp (#): (Optional) Add '#' for a sharp note (e.g., C#4).
  • Octave: A number from 0 to 8 representing the octave of the note.
  • Duration: A number representing the duration of the note in seconds.

Separate each note and duration pair with a comma. For example:

C4:1, D4:0.5, REST:0.5, E4:1

REST: Use "REST" to indicate a pause. Specify its duration in seconds (e.g., REST:1).

Examples:

  • C4:1 – Play the note C in the 4th octave for 1 second.
  • D#5:0.5 – Play the sharp D note in the 5th octave for 0.5 seconds.
  • REST:2 – Add a 2-second pause.
  • G3:0.5, A3:1 – Play G in the 3rd octave for 0.5 seconds, followed by A in the 3rd octave for 1 second.

Select the sound type or instrument from the dropdown menu before playing the tune.

Click "Play Tune" to hear your composition or "Play All Tunes Together" to combine all inputs.

About
